Resonance is a powerful local music player built entirely with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, designed for people who want full control over their listening experience — no ads, no subscriptions, completely FOSS forever.

<b>Playback</b>
* Gapless playback and configurable crossfade
* ReplayGain 2.0 with per-track/per-album normalization
* Skip silence, playback speed and independent pitch control
* History-aware smart shuffle and a sleep timer

<b>Library</b>
* Full MediaStore integration with folder browsing and excluded folders
* Configurable multi-artist tag delimiters
* Sort and filter by title, artist, album, date added, duration, and play count
* Persistent queue across restarts

<b>Streaming</b>
* Navidrome / Subsonic support — browse, stream, and scrobble from your self-hosted server
* Chromecast support

<b>Lyrics</b>
* Synced lyrics with LRC, TTML, and word-level karaoke support
* Reads embedded lyrics from ID3 tags
* Automatic online lookup via LRCLib
* Built-in lyrics editor

Resonance also supports peer-to-peer song sharing without an account, Last.fm scrobbling, and a Material You interface that adapts to your device's theme.
